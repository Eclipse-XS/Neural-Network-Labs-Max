QUERY: How do point-in-time correct joins in a feature store prevent data leakage during training dataset generation?
FILTER: None

============================================================
SIMILARITY RETRIEVER ANSWER:
============================================================
Point-in-time correct joins in a feature store prevent data leakage during training dataset generation by ensuring that only data available up to the observation timestamp $t_{\text{obs}}$ is used in feature computation. Specifically, the feature store evaluates the exact state of a feature as of $t_{\text{obs}}$, and any feature update occurring at a time $t > t_{\text{obs}}$ is explicitly excluded from the training tuple. This temporal restriction prevents the inclusion of future information in the training dataset, thereby avoiding temporal data leakage [02_feature_engineering.md].

This mechanism directly addresses the failure mode of temporal leakage—where normalization or feature computation spans the entire historical partition rather than being confined to the causal training window—ensuring that the training data reflects only the information that was available at the time of observation [01_data_quality.md]. As a result, the training dataset remains temporally consistent and causally valid, which is essential for building generalizable and reliable machine learning models.

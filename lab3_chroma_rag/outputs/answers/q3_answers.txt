Covariate data drift differs from concept drift in the nature of the distribution shift:

- **Covariate drift** occurs when the marginal input feature distribution changes over time, while the conditional relationship between inputs and targets remains constant: $P_t(X) \neq P_0(X)$, while $P_t(Y|X) = P_0(Y|X)$ [06_model_monitoring.md|ID:06_model_monitoring_chunk_001].
- **Concept drift** manifests when the underlying relationship between inputs and targets shifts fundamentally: $P_t(Y|X) \neq P_0(Y|X)$, while $P_t(X) \approx P_0(X)$ [06_model_monitoring.md|ID:06_model_monitoring_chunk_002].

Regarding incident triage and diagnosis of distribution shifts:

This phase helps identify potential sources of covariate drift or data quality degradation that may be contributing to observed distribution shifts in production models.

Thus, while the monitoring framework detects statistical drift via divergence metrics (e.g., PSI) across time windows [06_model_monitoring.md|ID:06_model_monitoring_chunk_003], incident triage focuses on root cause analysis by auditing upstream data pipelines to validate the origin of distribution shifts.

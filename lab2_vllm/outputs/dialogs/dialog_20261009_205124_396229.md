## User

What is overfitting?

## Assistant

Overfitting is when a model learns to perform very well on the training data but fails to generalize and make accurate predictions for new, unseen data. It's like trying to memorize every detail of a test question instead of understanding the main idea.

Imagine you're drawing pictures based only on what your friend tells you about their day without looking at any photos or videos. You might draw some details correctly but not capture the overall story. This is similar to how an overfitted model works with data: it captures too many patterns from the training set rather than generalizing to new examples.

To avoid this, we need to find a balance between having enough information (training data) to learn effectively and not capturing all possible variations (overfitting). Techniques like regularization can help by adding a penalty for complexity, making sure the model doesn't become overly complex and thus prone to overfitting.
